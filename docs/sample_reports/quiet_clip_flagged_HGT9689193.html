How the manipulation type is chosen (rules, first match wins)
  1. uncertain: no decodable signal or no evidence at all
  2. spliced/edited: a splice/discontinuity expert reports a seam with strength >= 0.6 (a local edit is the most specific explanation, even inside a synthetic clip)
  3. replay: an environment/replay finding (reverb, loudspeaker, re-recording) is synthetic-leaning with strength >= 0.6
  4. voice conversion: cm-score >= synthetic threshold AND (speaker-consistency flags OR prosody evidence leans natural while spectral/deep evidence leans synthetic: VC keeps the source talker's prosody but replaces the timbre)
  5. TTS / neural vocoder: cm-score >= synthetic threshold otherwise (spectral, vocoder, deep or prosody evidence of generated speech)
  6. laundered (transcoded/band-limited/noise): compression-history evidence >= 0.5, or a lossy / band-limited file, while the score is below the synthetic threshold (processing may be hiding artefacts)
  7. bona fide: cm-score <= bona fide threshold and no synthetic finding reaches the strong-flag level
  8. uncertain: anything else
